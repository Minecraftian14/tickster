# Product

## Purpose

We are building an experimental investment-intelligence laboratory: a disciplined environment where ideas about machine-assisted investment research are tested rigorously and honestly.

Its central question is:

> Which components of machine-assisted investment research, if any, produce statistically and economically significant incremental value over simpler quantitative and human baselines, once realistic costs, biases, and uncertainties are
> accounted for?

The product exists to answer that question with credible evidence — not to be an impressive trading bot.
Success is measured by what it can reliably tell us: what was known, what was believed, what was predicted, what was decided, what happened, and what was actually learned.

## Goals

1. **Rigorous, falsifiable research.** Evaluate machine-assisted research methods against simple quantitative and human baselines, with honest treatment of costs, uncertainty, and research bias. Negative results are valid outcomes.
2. **A laboratory that catches its own mistakes.** Resist false discoveries, hindsight, and contaminated evaluations, so that conclusions can be trusted.
3. **Epistemic accountability.** Keep the path from observation to knowledge intact: evidence, forecasts, decisions, outcomes, and lessons remain distinct, traceable, and reproducible.
4. **Decision support under human control.** Produce research recommendations that inform decisions; real-world action remains subject to human judgment and risk oversight.
5. **Earned complexity.** Every capability must demonstrate measurable value. What does not justify its existence is simplified or removed.
6. **Incremental delivery.** Establish a trustworthy foundation first; adopt advanced techniques — including LLMs — only as measured experiments built on top of it.

## Non-Goals

- **Autonomous trading.** The product does not act on capital on its own; actions pass through human judgment and risk oversight.
- **Assuming LLMs produce alpha.** LLMs are hypotheses to be tested, not a committed source of value. A finding that they do not help is a successful outcome.
- **A fixed market or strategy commitment.** The initial focus (liquid equities, long-only, short-to-medium swing horizons) is provisional and may be revised or abandoned on evidence.
- **Technology showcase.** Advanced architectures are not goals in themselves; impressive demonstrations without economic or scientific value do not count as progress.
- **Immediate live operation.** The product matures gradually from historical study toward human-supervised use; reaching live markets early is not an objective.
