# data_enrichment 0.3.0 — Fundamental Enrichment

## Purpose

This milestone turns collected `FundamentalSnapshot` records into deterministic, provider-neutral financial analytics. The enrichment layer never fetches upstream financial data and never makes predictive or trading decisions.

## Internal normalization

`data_enrichment.fundamental.common` recognizes conceptual metrics through aliases such as revenue, EBITDA, EBIT, net income, EPS, assets, equity, debt, cash, operating cash flow, and capex. Same-period income statement, balance-sheet, and cash-flow records are merged into an analytical period while keeping metric-specific source lineage.

The merged view is internal to the enrichment package; the foundation's canonical records remain unchanged.

## Enrichers

- `fundamental.growth`: QoQ/YoY growth with gap checks appropriate to quarterly/yearly frequency.
- `fundamental.ttm`: trailing-four-quarter flow metrics, requiring four consecutive quarter periods.
- `fundamental.profitability`: gross/EBITDA/EBIT/net margins plus ROE and ROA. ROE/ROA use average current/prior balance-sheet denominators when available.
- `fundamental.solvency`: debt/equity, net debt/EBITDA, current ratio, quick ratio, and interest coverage.
- `fundamental.cashflow`: free cash flow, operating cash-flow margin, FCF margin, cash-flow-to-net-income, and capex intensity.

## Temporal semantics

Each derived series point carries `available_at`, computed conservatively from the records actually supporting that metric. This prevents a derived financial fact from appearing earlier than its latest required source input.

## Lineage semantics

Lineage is metric-specific. For example, revenue TTM cites the revenue source records for its four quarters, while ROE cites current net income plus current and prior equity records. Unrelated statements from the same period are not included merely because they were filed together.

## Calculation conventions

- Growth: `current / comparison - 1`.
- TTM: sum of the latest four consecutive quarterly flow values.
- Margins: numerator divided by same-period revenue.
- ROE: net income divided by average current/prior equity when both are available.
- ROA: net income divided by average current/prior assets when both are available.
- Net debt: debt minus cash.
- Quick assets: current assets minus inventory.
- Interest coverage: EBIT divided by absolute interest expense.
- FCF when not supplied: operating cash flow minus absolute capex.

These conventions are explicit metadata on derived series and can be replaced or extended by future specialized enrichers.
